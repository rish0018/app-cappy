Apple’s editorial illustrations, and the welcoming friendliness of
Pixar while remaining entirely original and visually consistent across
the Cappy ecosystem.”

RECOMMENDED FOLDER STRUCTURE
=====================================================================
Library for Achievements Section 
This is one of those ideas that can either become a gimmick or become the thing people remember about Cappy ten years from now.

The difference is whether it feels like:

> "A bookshelf UI with badges"

or

> "A physical place that slowly becomes a reflection of your learning journey."

You want the second.

---

# The Core Philosophy

Most apps treat achievements as events.

```text
Achievement unlocked.
+50 XP.
Badge added.
```

Then it disappears forever.

Cappy should treat achievements as memories.

A learner shouldn't think:

> "I unlocked a badge."

They should think:

> "I wrote another chapter."

The library is not an achievement screen.

It is your learning autobiography.

---

# The Feeling

If Duolingo is:

```text
Arcade machine
```

Cappy should feel like:

```text
Personal study room.
```

Imagine entering a quiet library at sunset.

Dust particles float through warm light.

Books sit on oak shelves.

A small lamp glows softly.

The room feels lived in.

Not empty.

Not gamified.

Not optimized for engagement metrics.

Optimized for reflection.

Not a game.

# Why 3D Matters

Flat cards say:

```text
data
```

Physical objects say:

```text
history
```

A 3D book immediately communicates:

* weight
* permanence
* ownership
* value

You remember books.

You don't remember cards.

---

Not realistic Unreal Engine rendering.

Think:

```text

+
Pixar props
```

Materials should be stylized realism.
