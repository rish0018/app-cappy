CAPPY BRAND ASSET PROMPT LIBRARY v1.0

Purpose

This document contains a reusable prompt library for generating a
consistent visual identity for Cappy.

=====================================================================
GLOBAL BRAND ART DIRECTION (Append this to EVERY prompt)
=====================================================================

Art Direction: Maintain a consistent visual identity for the Cappy
brand.

Color Palette - Primary Blue: #4F7EA8 - Warm Sand: #D9AA78 - Soft Beige:
#CEC1AE - Sage Green: #8AB8AE - Deep Teal: #3E948C - Warm Walnut Brown:
#7A5438 - Cream White: #F8F4EE

Color Ratios 50% Warm Cream / Beige 20% Soft Blue 15% Natural Wood
Browns 10% Sage Green 5% Brass / Copper

Lighting Warm morning or golden-hour sunlight. Soft ambient lighting. No
harsh shadows. No neon. No cyberpunk.

Materials Natural oak wood Matte ceramic Leather journals Brass accents
Linen Paper textures Soft wool Indoor plants

Environment Scandinavian-inspired educational interiors. Minimal
clutter. Natural textures. Calm and welcoming.

Illustration Style Premium editorial illustration. Semi-realistic
painterly rendering. Pixar-quality character design. Rounded forms. Soft
expressions. High detail. Never photorealistic.

Mood Calm Encouraging Thoughtful Curious Optimistic Peaceful

Avoid Neon colors Plastic materials Corporate stock illustration Visual
clutter Comic-book outlines Anime styling Overly childish expressions

Final Style Anchor “The final artwork should feel like it belongs to a
premium accessibility education brand, combining the calm warmth of
Headspace, the craftsmanship of Studio Ghibli backgrounds, the polish of
Apple’s editorial illustrations, and the welcoming friendliness of
Pixar while remaining entirely original and visually consistent across
the Cappy ecosystem.”
