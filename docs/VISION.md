# Vision — Cappy

## The short version
Cappy exists to make accessibility-based communication easier to learn, practice, and remember.

## Four guiding questions
1. Who is this for? Learners who want a calm, accessible way to study communication systems such as ASL.
2. What problem are we solving? Many learners need guided feedback and practice, not just static lessons.
3. Why now? Camera-based validation and modern cross-platform tools make real-time learning experiences practical.
4. How will success be measured? Better lesson completion, better retention, and meaningful learner progress.

## Product Direction
- Keep the experience simple and approachable.
- Prioritize educational value over novelty.
- Build reusable foundations for future languages and systems.
